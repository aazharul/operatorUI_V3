A state line (bypass, then discharge, then cover, then power), then four tiles: Discharge against the consent, Flow, Cover and the shortest Runway, with energy, reuse and uptime behind Show more. The trust summary is the tile block’s label. Stage rows follow in flow order, each with its state and one worst KPI. The Upkeep line (PM on time as a share of due occurrences, C-22) sits in the work zone. “N issues ›” and “N tasks ›” in the header open the two tabs for this place (D-131); the card itself lists no work. Tiles are marked PROPOSAL (Mansi: plant and stage are still being worked out). Operator lens: acts on everything in reach.
